NEXUS is currently offline.
Here's what happened and what it means for your data.
// Google Cloud — Billing Suspension Notice
// Why NEXUS is down Google suspended the Firebase project powering NEXUS due to a billing account violation. Keeping NEXUS on Firebase requires an ongoing paid plan — and the cost is too high to continue on their infrastructure.
// What this means for your data Because the account is suspended before any export could happen, it is not possible to retrieve any existing messages or accounts. This means a full reset is unavoidable — all data will be lost. There is currently no way around this.
// What happens next NEXUS will be rebuilt on self-hosted infrastructure — no Google, no cloud bills. When it comes back it will be a fresh start. We'll update this page when it's live again.
// Help shape what comes next While NEXUS is rebuilding, we'd love to hear from you — what you liked, what you didn't, and what you want to see when it returns. ✦ Take the Survey ✦
Sorry for the inconvenience. Thanks for your patience and for using NEXUS.
